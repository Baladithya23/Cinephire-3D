- **Languages Verified:** Telugu and English.
- **Showtimes (Sample Slots):** 
  - Telugu: 11:00 AM, 6:20 PM, 10:00 PM
  - English: 12:35 PM, 7:20 PM, 10:50 PM

### 2. Capital Cinemas, Trendset Mall
- **Verified Data:** Trendset Mall, MG Road, opposite HDFC Bank, Kala Nagar, Benz Circle, Vijayawada 520010. 6 screens. Dolby Atmos confirmed. 3D projection. 108 luxury recliners with push-back seating. Free Wi-Fi, fully air-conditioned.
- **Languages Verified:** Telugu and Hindi.
- **Showtimes (Sample Slots):** 
  - Telugu (Screen 1): 1:45 PM, 4:50 PM, 7:40 PM, 10:30 PM
  - Hindi (Screen 6): 1:20 PM, 7:35 PM, 10:45 PM
- **Missing / Needs Audit:** Total seat capacity not found.
- **Critical Myth Clarification:** Early construction rumors claimed an IMAX screen with Auro 11.1; no evidence indicates an IMAX screen was ever commissioned. Do not market as IMAX.

## 4. Single-Screen & Historic Heritage Halls

The reference document records surviving historic halls:
- **Durga Kalamandiram:** Built in 1936; one of the two oldest surviving single-screen cinema halls in Vijayawada.
- **Rajakumari:** The second historic single-screen hall still operational in Vijayawada.
- **Swarna Palace:** 3-screen complex on the historic Lakshmi Talkies site.
- **Apsara Cinema House (Governorpet)**, **Ravi Cinemas (PNBS)**, **Sowmya Theatre (Chittinagar)**, **Raj Cinema Hall (Vijayawada)**.
- **Balaji Iconia A/C 2K Dolby Surround (Ibrahimpatnam):** Dolby Surround, 2K; located outside the municipal perimeter.

## 5. Pricing & Regulatory Policy (Andhra Pradesh Context)

- **Government Cap Base (Vijayawada AC Multiplexes):** ₹75, ₹150, ₹250 (Slabs based on municipal baseline order).
- **Blockbuster / Special Releases:** ₹377 regular seats; ₹495 recliners; peak special ticketing up to ₹600.
- **Site Policy:** All displayed prices are clearly flagged as **"Demo Sample Pricing"**. The application does not represent estimated prices as official current box-office rates. Exact final payable amounts are calculated dynamically by the payment backend during checkout.
